1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application Snake Hunt (Android package ID: com.TheDataSequence.SnakeHunt), developed and offered by The Data Sequence ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to transparency regarding privacy practices when you download, install, access, or play Snake Hunt on your mobile device.

2. Introduction

Snake Hunt is a mobile arcade action game designed for Android devices and distributed through the Google Play Store.

The game allows players to control a cannon, aim at snake segments, fire projectiles, destroy enemy segments, collect special abilities, progress through levels, and attempt to defeat increasingly challenging snakes.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when someone uses the App, including information processed by integrated third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using Snake Hunt. By downloading, accessing, or playing the App, you acknowledge the privacy practices described in this document. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the App.

3. Information We May Collect

Snake Hunt does not require users to create an account, provide a name, submit an email address, or enter personal profile information in order to play the game.

Depending on your device settings, network environment, and integrated third-party services, certain technical and advertising-related information may nevertheless be processed automatically.

Potential categories of information that may be processed include:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, screen characteristics, hardware properties, and system configuration.
  • Operating System Information: Android version and related operating system information.
  • App Information: App version, build information, package identifier, and installation source.
  • Language & Region: Device language and general regional settings.
  • IP Address & Network Information: IP address, connection type, network information, and related technical information processed by advertising services.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID, subject to device settings and applicable privacy requirements.
  • Advertising Information: Information relating to advertisements displayed, viewed, interacted with, or measured through Google AdMob.
  • Diagnostic Information: Technical error information or diagnostic information where provided by integrated services.
Important Distinction: Not all categories listed above are collected directly by the developer. Some information may be processed automatically by third-party services, particularly Google AdMob, in order to deliver and measure advertisements and maintain advertising security.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you launch and use Snake Hunt, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the Android operating system and integrated third-party SDKs.

In particular, Google AdMob may automatically process information necessary to request, display, measure, and protect advertisements.

The developer does not ask players to manually provide this technical information during normal gameplay.

5. Device and Technical Information

Technical and device information may be processed for operational, advertising, compatibility, security, and troubleshooting purposes.

Such information may include:

  • Device manufacturer and model.
  • Android operating system version.
  • Screen and display characteristics.
  • App version and build information.
  • General network and connectivity information.
  • IP address where processed by third-party services.
  • Advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Technical information required for advertisement delivery and fraud prevention.

This information may be used to help ensure that advertisements and app functionality operate correctly across different Android devices.

6. Gameplay and Usage Information

Snake Hunt stores certain gameplay and progression information locally on the player's device to provide game functionality.

This may include:

  • Current level and unlocked level progression.
  • Gameplay score and related progress.
  • Cannon life state during gameplay.
  • Power-up and ability progression or discovery state.
  • Gameplay settings and locally stored preferences.
  • Other locally stored information required to continue gameplay between sessions.

This information is used to provide progression, gameplay functionality, rewards, and other features available within the App.

7. Local Gameplay Data

The App uses local device storage to preserve certain gameplay and progression information between sessions.

Local information may include current level progression, unlocked levels, gameplay progress, ability discovery state, settings, and other game-progress information required by Snake Hunt.

This local gameplay information is not intended to identify you personally and is not a user account or cloud profile.

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored gameplay information may be deleted by the Android operating system.

8. Analytics Information

Snake Hunt does not currently require a separate user account or user-submitted personal profile for gameplay.

The App may receive or process technical information through third-party services used to operate advertising functionality, measure advertisement performance, prevent fraud, and maintain service reliability.

We do not intentionally use the App to collect names, postal addresses, telephone numbers, passwords, or other directly identifying information from players during normal gameplay.

9. Advertising

Snake Hunt is supported in part through advertising.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements where applicable.

Advertising providers may process technical and advertising-related information to:

  • Deliver and display advertisements.
  • Measure advertisement impressions and performance.
  • Measure interactions with advertisements.
  • Provide rewarded advertising experiences when a player chooses to watch a rewarded advertisement.
  • Prevent fraudulent or invalid advertising activity.
  • Maintain advertising security and service reliability.
  • Control advertising frequency.
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements depending on applicable laws, consent, and device settings.

Advertisements may be personalized where permitted by applicable law and according to the user's choices and advertising settings. Where personalization is not permitted or the required consent is not available, advertising services may provide non-personalized advertisements instead.

Depending on the user's device configuration, location, applicable laws, consent status, and Google's advertising systems, AdMob may process information such as:

  • Security: To help prevent advertising fraud, invalid traffic, abuse, and unauthorized activity.

  • Product Improvement: To improve game functionality, design, performance, and player experience.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal obligations, and lawful requests.
