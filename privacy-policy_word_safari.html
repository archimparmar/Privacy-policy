1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled in connection with Word Safari, a word-search puzzle game developed for Android devices.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about the information that may be processed when you download, install, access, or play Word Safari.

2. Introduction

Word Safari is a casual word-search puzzle game that allows players to discover hidden words through progressively challenging puzzles, safari-themed environments, daily puzzles, and gameplay challenges.

The game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, phone number, or home address in order to play.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of technical and usage information that may be processed by the game and by third-party services integrated into the application.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on your device, settings, region, and the third-party services used by the application, certain information may be processed automatically.

Potential categories include:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, screen characteristics, and hardware information.
  • Operating System Information: Android version and related system information.
  • Application Information: Application version, build information, and installation-related information.
  • Network Information: IP address, network type, and general connectivity information where processed by third-party services.
  • Advertising Information: Advertising identifiers and advertising-related information where applicable.
  • Gameplay Information: Game sessions, levels played, levels completed, feature usage, hints, and similar gameplay interactions where analytics or advertising services process such information.
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash information, error information, and technical performance information where provided by integrated services.
Important: Not every category listed above is necessarily collected directly by Word Safari. Some information may be processed automatically by third-party services used to provide advertising or other technical functionality.

4. Device and Technical Information

Technical information may be processed to help the application and its supporting services function properly.

This may include information such as:

  • Device model and manufacturer.
  • Android operating system version.
  • Application version and build information.
  • Screen and device configuration.
  • General network information.
  • Technical diagnostics and error information.

This information may be used to improve compatibility, stability, performance, security, and the overall player experience.

5. Gameplay Information

Word Safari may process general gameplay and interaction information when necessary for game functionality, diagnostics, advertising, or service improvement.

Examples may include:

  • Levels started and completed.
  • Game sessions and session duration.
  • Hints or other game features used.
  • Daily puzzle or challenge participation.
  • General navigation and interaction with game features.

Gameplay information is used to support the operation and improvement of the game and its features.

6. Advertising

Word Safari may display advertisements to support the continued development and availability of the game.

Advertisements may include banner, interstitial, or rewarded advertisements depending on the version and configuration of the application.

Advertising providers may process technical and advertising-related information in order to deliver advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalized or non-personalized advertising where permitted.

7. Google AdMob

Word Safari uses Google AdMob for in-app advertising.

Google AdMob is a service provided by Google that enables advertisements to be displayed inside mobile applications.

Depending on applicable laws, user consent, device settings, and Google's advertising configuration, Google may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device information.
  • IP address and network information.
  • Ad impressions and interactions.
  • General diagnostic and performance information.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit the official Google Privacy Policy.

8. Advertising Identifiers

Android devices may provide advertising identifiers that can be used by advertising services to support advertising delivery and measurement.

Users may be able to reset, restrict, or delete advertising identifiers through Android privacy and advertising settings, depending on their device and Android version.

Advertising choices may also depend on applicable regional consent requirements.

9. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with Word Safari may be used for purposes including:

  • Operating and maintaining the game.
  • Providing core gameplay functionality.
  • Improving game performance and compatibility.
  • Detecting and resolving technical problems.
  • Understanding general gameplay usage and improving game design.
  • Delivering and measuring advertisements.
  • Preventing advertising fraud and abuse.
  • Protecting the security and integrity of the application.
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements.

10. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide services used by the application.

These may include:

  • Google AdMob – advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.
  • Platform and infrastructure providers – technical services required to operate the application.

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

11. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Potential Information
Google AdMob In-app advertising and advertisement measurement. Advertising identifier, device information, IP/network information, ad interactions, and related technical information.

12. Data Retention

Word Safari does not maintain traditional user accounts or user profiles requiring personal registration.

Information processed by third-party services such as advertising providers may be retained according to their own privacy policies, retention schedules, and applicable legal requirements.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or alteration.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Children's Privacy

Word Safari is a general-audience word puzzle game.

We do not knowingly request or collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable children's privacy laws.

Where advertising services are used, their handling of information relating to children is subject to their own policies, applicable laws, and consent requirements.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information inappropriately may contact us using the contact details provided below.

15. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers used by Word Safari may operate infrastructure in multiple countries.

As a result, information processed by these providers may be transferred to or processed in countries other than the country in which you live.

Such processing is subject to the applicable privacy practices and legal safeguards of the relevant service provider.

16. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and Android device, you may have choices regarding advertising and related data processing.

  • Manage advertising settings through Android device settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier where supported.
  • Manage advertising consent where a consent mechanism is presented.
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

17. Data Deletion Requests

Word Safari does not require account registration or login to play. Therefore, we do not maintain a traditional personal user account database for gameplay.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information that may be associated with your use of the application, you may contact us using the contact information below.

Requests involving information processed by third-party services may need to be handled directly by those service providers, depending on the nature of the information and applicable law.

18. Third-Party Links

Advertisements or other content displayed through third-party services may lead to external websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites and services.

Please review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

19. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Word Safari, advertising services, technical services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest information.
