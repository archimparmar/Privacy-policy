1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application Word Adventure (Android package ID: com.wordpuzzle.wordadventure), developed and offered by The Data Sequence ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to transparency regarding privacy practices when you download, install, access, or play Word Adventure on your mobile device.

2. Introduction

Word Adventure is a fantasy-themed word puzzle game for Android devices distributed through the Google Play Store.

The game allows players to solve vocabulary and word puzzles, progress through adventure worlds, complete daily challenges, play mini-games, earn Coins and Diamonds, use hints, unlock achievements, maintain streaks, and participate in rewarded advertising opportunities.

The App may also provide push notifications for purposes such as Daily Challenges, streak reminders, gameplay reminders, and other relevant game-related notifications.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when someone uses the App, including information processed by integrated third-party services such as Google AdMob and Firebase Cloud Messaging where applicable.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using Word Adventure. By downloading, accessing, or playing the App, you acknowledge the privacy practices described in this document. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the App.

3. Information We May Collect

Word Adventure does not require users to create an account, provide a name, submit an email address, or enter personal profile information in order to play the game.

Depending on your device settings, network environment, app configuration, and integrated third-party services, certain technical, advertising, notification, and diagnostic information may nevertheless be processed automatically.

Potential categories of information may include:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, screen characteristics, hardware properties, and system configuration.
  • Operating System Information: Android version and related operating system information.
  • App Information: App version, build information, package identifier, and installation source.
  • Language and Region: Device language and general regional settings.
  • IP Address and Network Information: IP address, connection type, network information, and related technical information processed by third-party services.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers such as the Google Advertising ID, subject to device settings and applicable requirements.
  • Advertising Information: Information relating to advertisements displayed, viewed, completed, interacted with, or measured through Google AdMob.
  • Notification Information: Device or app identifiers and notification-related technical information necessary for push notification delivery where Firebase Cloud Messaging is used.
  • Diagnostic Information: Technical errors, crashes, or diagnostic information where provided by integrated services.
Important Distinction: Not all categories listed above are collected directly by the developer. Some information may be processed automatically by third-party services, particularly advertising and notification services, to provide their respective functionality.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you launch and use Word Adventure, certain technical information may be processed automatically by the Android operating system and integrated third-party SDKs.

Google AdMob may process information necessary to request, display, measure, and protect advertisements. Firebase Cloud Messaging may process technical information necessary to deliver push notifications to supported devices.

The developer does not ask players to manually provide this technical information during normal gameplay.

5. Device and Technical Information

Technical and device information may be processed for operational, advertising, compatibility, security, notification delivery, and troubleshooting purposes.

  • Device manufacturer and model.
  • Android operating system version.
  • Screen and display characteristics.
  • App version and build information.
  • General network and connectivity information.
  • IP address where processed by third-party services.
  • Advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Technical information required for advertisement delivery and fraud prevention.
  • Technical information required for push notification delivery.

6. Gameplay and Usage Information

Word Adventure stores certain gameplay and progression information locally on the player's device to provide game functionality.

This may include:

  • Current main-game level.
  • Main-game progression.
  • Daily Challenge progress.
  • Daily streak information.
  • Mini-game progression for Crossword, Word Riddles, and Word Search.
  • XP and player progression.
  • Coin balance.
  • Diamond balance.
  • Hint inventory.
  • Achievements and milestones.
  • Bonus word discoveries.
  • Hard word discoveries.
  • Gameplay statistics.
  • Best and current streak information.
  • Tutorial completion status.

This information is used to provide progression, rewards, statistics, challenges, achievements, streaks, and other gameplay features.

7. Local Gameplay Data

The App uses local device storage to preserve certain gameplay and progression information between sessions.

Local information may include player level, XP, Coins, Diamonds, hints, achievements, daily challenges, streaks, statistics, tutorial status, mini-game progress, and other game-progress information.

This local gameplay information is not intended to identify you personally and is not a user account or cloud profile.

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored gameplay information may be deleted by the Android operating system.

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements and other advertising formats where applicable.

Rewarded advertisements may allow players to receive certain in-game rewards, such as Coins, Diamonds, or other available rewards, after successfully completing an eligible advertisement.

Advertising providers may process technical and advertising-related information to:

  • Deliver and display advertisements.
  • Provide rewarded advertisements.
  • Measure advertisement impressions and performance.
  • Measure interactions with advertisements.
  • Prevent fraudulent or invalid advertising activity.
  • Maintain advertising security and service reliability.
  • Control advertising frequency.
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements depending on applicable laws, consent, and device settings.

Advertisements may be personalized where permitted by applicable law and according to the user's choices and advertising settings.

10. Advertising Identifiers

Subject to applicable laws, device configuration, and user choices, advertising identifiers may be used to:

Users can manage advertising-related settings through privacy and advertising controls available on their Android device and, where applicable, through Google privacy controls.

11. Push Notifications

Word Adventure may use push notifications to provide useful game-related reminders and updates.

Notifications may include reminders about:

  • Daily Challenges.
  • Daily streaks.
  • Returning to the game.
  • Other relevant gameplay events or updates.

Push notifications may be provided using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Where FCM is used, technical information such as an app/device registration token may be processed to deliver notifications to the appropriate device.

The App requests notification permission where required by the operating system and applicable platform requirements.

You may disable notifications through the App's notification setting where available and/or through your Android device notification settings.

We do not use push notifications to request passwords, payment credentials, or other sensitive personal information.

Information processed in connection with Word Adventure may be used for the following purposes:

  • Gameplay Progression: To preserve levels, achievements, rewards, streaks, challenges, statistics, and mini-game progress.
  • Advertising: To request, display, measure, and manage advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Rewarded Ads: To provide eligible in-game rewards after successful completion of rewarded advertisements.
  • Notifications: To deliver Daily Challenge, streak, reminder, and other game-related notifications.
  • Performance: To maintain compatibility and reliable operation across Android devices.
  • Security: To help prevent advertising fraud, invalid traffic, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
  • Troubleshooting: To investigate technical problems where relevant technical information is available.
  • Product Improvement: To improve game functionality, design, performance, and player experience.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, legal obligations, and lawful requests.
