1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application Pawzzle, developed and offered by The Data Sequence ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to transparency regarding privacy practices when you download, install, access, or play Pawzzle on your mobile device.

2. Introduction

Pawzzle is a colorful and relaxing block puzzle game designed for mobile devices.

Players place different block shapes onto an 8 × 8 puzzle grid, complete horizontal rows and vertical columns, clear blocks, create combinations, and achieve higher scores.

Pawzzle also includes gameplay features such as levels, collectible items, decorative block themes, daily rewards, missions, in-game coins, gems, lives, and player customization.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when someone uses the App, including information processed by integrated third-party advertising services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using Pawzzle. By using the App, you acknowledge the privacy practices described in this document.

3. Information We May Collect

Pawzzle does not require players to submit an email address, telephone number, or password to access its core puzzle gameplay.

Players may optionally enter a display name through the in-game profile feature.

Depending on device settings and integrated services, the following categories of information may be processed:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, screen characteristics, and hardware configuration.
  • Operating System: Android or iOS version.
  • Application Information: App version, build number, and installation information.
  • Language and Region: Device language and general regional settings.
  • Network Information: IP address and connection information where processed by third-party services.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Advertising Activity: Advertisement impressions, interactions, and related measurement information.
  • Gameplay Information: Scores, levels, collections, rewards, progress, and in-game balances.
  • Player Display Name: An optional name selected by the player within the game.
  • Diagnostic Information: Technical errors and performance information where provided by integrated services.
Important Distinction: Not all information listed above is necessarily collected directly by the developer. Some technical information may be processed automatically by third-party services used within the App.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you launch and use Pawzzle, certain technical information may be processed automatically by your device's operating system and integrated third-party services.

If advertising services are enabled, they may process information necessary to request, display, measure, and protect advertisements.

Players are not required to manually provide this technical information during normal gameplay.

5. Device and Technical Information

Technical and device information may be processed for compatibility, advertising, security, and troubleshooting.

  • Device manufacturer and model.
  • Operating system and version.
  • Screen and display characteristics.
  • Application version and build information.
  • General network and connectivity information.
  • IP address where processed by third-party services.

This information may help maintain reliable application functionality and advertising performance.

6. Gameplay and Usage Information

Pawzzle stores certain gameplay and progression information to provide its puzzle features and maintain progress.

This information may include:

  • Current and best scores.
  • Current puzzle board state.
  • Block placements and gameplay progression.
  • Completed and unlocked levels.
  • Collected fruits, animals, treasures, and other items.
  • Unlocked block themes and customization selections.
  • In-game coin and gem balances.
  • Available lives and reward balances.
  • Daily reward and mission progress.
  • Gameplay achievements and milestones.
  • Player-selected display name.
  • Music, sound effect, and vibration preferences.

This information is used to operate gameplay, preserve progress, display collections, provide rewards, and support customization.

7. Local Gameplay Data

Pawzzle is designed to support offline puzzle gameplay. Gameplay and progression information may be stored locally on the player's device.

Locally stored information may include scores, puzzle board states, levels, collectibles, player preferences, rewards, in-game balances, and the selected player display name.

This information is used to restore gameplay and maintain progress between sessions.

Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling Pawzzle may remove locally stored gameplay information, subject to any device backup or restoration features.

8. Analytics Information

Pawzzle may process technical or performance-related information through integrated services used to maintain app functionality, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, or investigate errors.

Any separate analytics or crash-reporting service included in the App will be subject to its applicable privacy practices.

We do not require players to provide postal addresses, telephone numbers, or passwords for ordinary gameplay.
